HR LeadFlow is my entrepreneurial venture focused on lead generation and helping businesses connect with potential customers.

Our current direction is focused on the solar industry in Pune and surrounding areas. The idea is to identify potential customers and connect them with relevant solar service providers.

Building this has introduced me to the practical side of business: prospect research, customer conversations, outreach, qualification and figuring out how to build a reliable process.
